Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the Pomodoro Pets: Focus Timer app ("Pomodoro Pets", "we", "us") collects, why, and what choices you have. Pomodoro Pets is provided by Generated Media, Inc.

The short version

  • Pomodoro Pets has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your settings, tasks, focus history, creatures and flowberry balance are stored on your device. We have no servers of our own; the usage and technical information described below goes to our analytics and advertising providers.
  • We use Amplitude and Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used. Analytics events do not contain the names of your tasks.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Pomodoro Pets Pro removes them. Ads use your advertising identifier only if you allow tracking.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your focus and break settings, tasks and their names, the history of your focus sessions, your creatures and their levels, and your flowberry balance are saved on your device. Timer notifications, alarms and the Live Activity on your Lock Screen are created on the device; we do not use push notifications. Deleting the app deletes this information, and we cannot restore it.

Information we collect

Usage analytics

The app uses Amplitude and Google Firebase Analytics to count how features are used: for example, that a focus session was started or completed and how long it was, whether a task was chosen (not its name), that a creature was fed or leveled up, that the paywall was shown, a product was selected or a purchase was completed, canceled or restored, and that an ad was shown. Firebase also records standard events such as the first app open, sessions and in-app purchases. These services also collect standard technical information, such as an app instance or device identifier, the identifier for vendor (IDFV), device model, operating system, app version, language, and an approximate location derived from your IP address (country or city). If you allow tracking (see below), Firebase may also receive your device's advertising identifier.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows full-screen ads between focus cycles and optional rewarded ads that give you flowberry. The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google collects information such as your device's advertising identifier (if you allow it), IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with ads. Google's use of this information is described in its How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • The app asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not available to us or Google, and ads are not personalized based on your activity in other companies' apps and websites. Google may still show ads based on the context of the app.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app may ask for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalized.
  • Pomodoro Pets Pro removes ads.

Purchases

Pomodoro Pets Pro subscriptions and flowberry and creature purchases are processed by Apple. We receive confirmation of what you bought from Apple to unlock it in the app, but we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details.

How we use information

  • To understand and improve how the app is used.
  • To show ads in the free version and measure them.
  • To provide paid features you bought.

We do not sell personal information. Ads may be personalized only if you allow tracking and, where required, give consent. Usage analytics run whether or not you allow tracking, based on our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app; they do not use the advertising identifier unless you allow tracking.

Service providers

We use these providers: Amplitude and Google Firebase for analytics, Google AdMob for ads, and Apple for purchases. They process information under their own privacy policies and their agreements with us.

How long we keep information

Information on your device stays there until you delete it or the app. Analytics data is kept only as long as we need it to understand how the app is used, within the retention periods set in our Amplitude and Google Analytics projects. Google keeps advertising data according to its own policies.

Your choices

  • Allow or stop tracking at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Remove ads by subscribing to Pomodoro Pets Pro.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information and to object to certain processing. Analytics data is linked only to random app and device identifiers, not to your name or email, so we may not be able to find it from a request alone; deleting the app resets the identifier Firebase uses for you, while Amplitude may keep using the same identifier for vendor (IDFV) while another app from us is installed on your device. Contact us to use these rights; we will respond within the time required by law.

Children

Pomodoro Pets is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

International transfers

We and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or want to use your rights, contact Generated Media, Inc. at support@icons8.com.

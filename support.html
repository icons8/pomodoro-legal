Frequently asked questions

How do I change the focus and break length?

Open Settings and choose the focus, short break and long break length. You can also turn on Auto-start next focus there.

How do I earn flowberry?

Each completed 25 minutes of focus earns flowberry. Use it to feed your creatures and help them grow. Canceled sessions do not earn flowberry.

Why did my progress disappear?

Pomodoro Pets has no accounts: your tasks, history, creatures and flowberry are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them, and we cannot restore them.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, or tap Restore on the Pomodoro Pets Pro screen. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe. Flowberry and creature purchases are used up in the app and cannot be restored on another device.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Pomodoro Pets and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, or before a free trial ends, to avoid being charged.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple too. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
